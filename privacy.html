It covers the web app and, in the future, any mobile version distributed through direct download or app stores. **Note on this document:** this policy was drafted to accurately describe how the app is actually built today. It is a strong starting point, not a substitute for review by a lawyer — particularly before accepting payments at scale or serving users in regions with specific data-protection laws (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA in California). Who this policy applies to This policy applies to anyone who uses Classic CV Builder, whether as a signed-in account holder or as a guest (not signed in). **Data controller:** koko, tamunoiminabo adokiye **Contact:** kokoiminabo@gmail.com --- ## 2. What information we collect ### If you use the app as a guest (not signed in) Your CVs and cover letters are stored **only in your own browser**, on your own device (using browser local storage). This data is never sent to any server, **except**: - when you use an AI-assisted writing feature (which requires signing in — see below), or - when you export a PDF, which happens entirely on your device via your browser's own print function and is never uploaded anywhere. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, guest documents are lost — they are not backed up anywhere by us. ### If you create an account - **Email address** — used to identify your account and for sign-in. - **Password** — stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash (bcrypt). We cannot see or recover your actual password, and we never store it in plain text. - **CV and cover letter content** — whatever personal, professional, or educational information you choose to enter (name, work history, education, skills, contact details, etc.), stored so you can access your documents from any device. - **Basic usage data** — which AI-assisted actions you've used and when, solely to apply free-plan usage limits and improve reliability. We do not track your activity across other websites or apps. ### If you subscribe to Premium Payment is handled entirely by **Stripe**. We do not receive, process, or store your card number or other payment details — Stripe stores that directly. We do store whether your account is on a paid plan, and a Stripe-issued customer/subscription reference so we can verify your subscription status. If a mobile version is published to Google Play, subscription purchases made there are verified using Google's Play Billing system in a similar way — we receive confirmation of your subscription status, not your payment details. --- ## 3. How we use your information - To provide the core service: creating, saving, editing, and exporting your CVs and cover letters. - To authenticate you and keep your account secure. - To generate AI-assisted writing suggestions when you request them. - To apply free-plan usage limits and determine Premium access. - To communicate with you about your account if needed (e.g. a security notice) — we do not send marketing email unless you're told otherwise and given a way to opt out. We do not sell your personal information to anyone, for any purpose. --- ## 4.
